========================================================================
             EMPIRICAL EXPLORATORY DATA ANALYSIS (EDA) FINDINGS          
========================================================================

Research Objective:
Investigate e-commerce customer behavior factors associated with non-conversion
and potential cart abandonment to inform targeted conversion optimization.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. OVERALL CONVERSION & SESSION DISTRIBUTION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Total Browsing Sessions  : 12,330
- Non-Converting Sessions  : 10,422 (84.53%)
- Converting Sessions      : 1,908 (15.47%)
- Class Imbalance Ratio    : 5.46 : 1
- Research Interpretation : Non-converting sessions (84.53%) represent potential
  abandonment and drop-off traffic where user intent was not fulfilled.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. PAGEVALUES OBSERVATIONS (STRONGEST EDA PREDICTOR)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Converting Sessions Mean PageValues    : 27.26
- Non-Converting Sessions Mean PageValues: 1.98
- Difference                             : +25.29
- Empirical Finding                      : High PageValues strongly coincide with
  completed purchases, whereas sessions with zero or near-zero PageValues account
  for the vast majority of non-conversions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. BOUNCE & EXIT RATES (DISENGAGEMENT METRICS)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Mean Bounce Rate (Non-Converting vs Converting): 0.0253 vs 0.0051
- Mean Exit Rate (Non-Converting vs Converting)  : 0.0474 vs 0.0196
- Empirical Finding                      : Non-converting sessions exhibited significantly
  higher average bounce and exit rates, indicating early drop-off before reaching checkout.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. PRODUCT ENGAGEMENT (PAGE VIEWS & DURATION)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Mean Product Page Views (Non-Converting vs Converting): 28.71 vs 48.21
- Mean Product Duration (sec) (Non-Converting vs Conv) : 1069.99 vs 1876.21
- Empirical Finding                      : Converting sessions engaged with substantially
  more product pages and spent nearly twice as long exploring products.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. CATEGORICAL & TEMPORAL PATTERNS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Highest Converting Visitor Group: New_Visitor (24.91% conversion rate)
- Highest Converting Month        : Nov (25.35% conversion rate)
- Weekend Conversion vs Weekday   : Weekday conversion rate is 14.89% vs Weekend 17.40%
